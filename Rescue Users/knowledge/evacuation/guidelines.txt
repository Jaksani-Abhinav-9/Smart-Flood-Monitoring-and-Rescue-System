==============================================================================
FLOOD RESCUE SYSTEM - CITIZEN EVACUATION GUIDELINES & SHELTER DIRECTORY
Official Evacuation Procedures, Go-Bag Checklist & Shelter Operations
==============================================================================

1. EVACUATION TRIGGER LEVELS & AUTHORITY DIRECTIVES
Citizens must recognize official emergency warning tiers issued by the District Collectorate and State Disaster Management Authority:
- Alert Level (Yellow): Water levels approaching bankfull or continuous heavy rainfall forecasted (> 64.5 mm in 24h). Monitor the FloodGuard app, test phone battery, inspect go-bags, and move ground-floor valuables upstairs.
- Warning Level (Orange): River or drainage overflowing into low-lying roads. Voluntary evacuation recommended for vulnerable citizens (pregnant women, elderly, infants, bedridden individuals).
- Danger / Evacuation Order Level (Red): Mandatory evacuation order declared. Inundation of residential neighborhoods is imminent. Evacuate immediately via designated high-elevation routes. Do not delay or wait until floodwaters surround your door.

2. PRE-IDENTIFIED RELIEF CAMPS & EVACUATION HUBS (KAMAREDDY DISTRICT CLUSTER)
The following government-designated emergency relief shelters provide dry accommodation, hot meals, safe drinking water, and basic medical care during flood disasters:
- Primary Shelter 1: Kamareddy Government High School & Junior College Campus, Station Road, Kamareddy (Capacity: 450 persons, Elevated concrete multi-story complex).
- Primary Shelter 2: Government Arts & Science Degree College, Nizamabad Road, Kamareddy (Capacity: 600 persons, Medical triage station on site).
- Primary Shelter 3: Collectorate Community Hall & Indoor Stadium, Housing Board Colony, Kamareddy (Capacity: 350 persons, Central district relief hub).
- Secondary Shelter 4: Zilla Parishad High School, Devanpally, Kamareddy Rural Sector (Capacity: 250 persons).
- Secondary Shelter 5: Municipal Town Hall, Gandhi Chowk, Kamareddy (Capacity: 200 persons).
For live shelter occupancy updates and nearest available camp allocation, consult the FloodGuard citizen map or contact the DEOC helpline at 1077.

3. 72-HOUR EMERGENCY EVACUATION GO-BAG (ESSENTIAL SURVIVAL CHECKLIST)
Every household should maintain a waterproof, easily portable backpack ("Go-Bag") ready to grab in under 60 seconds:
- Water: Minimum 3 liters of potable bottled water per person per day (sealed bottles), plus 1 packet of water purification tablets (chlorine/halogen).
- Food: Ready-to-eat, non-perishable high-calorie foods requiring no cooking or refrigeration (biscuits, dry fruits, energy bars, roasted nuts, canned beans).
- First Aid & Medicines: 7-day supply of critical prescription medications, sterile gauze bandages, adhesive strips, antiseptic cream, oral rehydration salts (ORS), and paracetamol.
- Lighting & Signaling: Waterproof LED flashlight with extra alkaline batteries, headlamp, and a pea-less plastic safety whistle (100+ decibels).
- Power & Connectivity: Fully charged portable power bank (10,000+ mAh), charging cables, and an emergency battery/hand-crank radio.
- Documents & Currency: Photocopies and waterproof-bagged original IDs (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Ration Card, property deeds, health insurance cards) and emergency cash in small denominations.
- Personal Hygiene: Moist towelettes/wipes, travel soap, toothbrush/paste, sanitary pads, toilet paper, and heavy-duty contractor trash bags for waterproofing gear.
- Infant Essentials: Baby formula, sterilized feeding bottles, baby clothing, and disposable diapers.

4. 5-MINUTE HOME SHUT-DOWN CHECKLIST BEFORE EVACUATING
If time permits before leaving your home:
1. Turn off the Main Circuit Breaker (MCB) switch at the electric panel box to prevent post-flood electrical fire and electrocution.
2. Turn off the main shutoff valve on your LPG cooking gas cylinder and disconnect the regulator hose.
3. Shut the main domestic water inlet valve to prevent contaminated floodwaters from entering household plumbing lines.
4. Move valuable electronic equipment, important books, and dry food items to the highest top shelves or upper floor.
5. Lock all exterior doors and windows securely. Leave a clear handwritten waterproof note on your front door stating: "EVACUATED TO [Camp Name] ON [Date/Time] - [X] Family Members Safe".
