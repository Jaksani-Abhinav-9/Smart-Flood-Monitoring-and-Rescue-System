==============================================================================
FLOOD RESCUE SYSTEM - KAMAREDDY LOCAL DISTRICT FLOOD GUIDE
Local Geographic Vulnerability Hotspots, High Ground Zones & Hospital Network
==============================================================================

1. KNOWN WATERLOGGING & INUNDATION HOTSPOTS (KAMAREDDY TOWN & SUBURBS)
During severe monsoon storms (> 65 mm in 24 hours), the following specific geographical locations in Kamareddy district face rapid water accumulation and road severance:
- Railway Underbridge (RUB) - Station Road: Deepest urban waterlogging basin in town. Accumulates 4 to 6 feet of runoff within 45 minutes of torrential rainfall. Submerges auto-rickshaws and two-wheelers. Never enter this underpass during heavy rain.
- Old Bus Stand & Vegetable Market Yard: Low-lying basin receiving runoff from higher commercial areas. Drains frequently back up with market silt.
- Gandhi Chowk & Ashok Nagar Main Arteries: High surface runoff causes rapid inundation of ground-floor shops and basements.
- Devanpally Nalla / Canal Overflows: In the semi-urban and rural fringe, the local feeder nalla overflows its banks, cutting off road transit to surrounding agricultural settlements.
- Nizam Sagar Catchment & Downstream Bunds: Villages situated downstream of the Nizam Sagar canal branches must monitor sluice discharge advisories from the Irrigation Department.

2. PRE-IDENTIFIED HIGH ELEVATION SAFE ZONES (ASSEMBLY HUBS)
If you must evacuate to high ground within Kamareddy town:
- Collectorate & Integrated District Offices Complex (IDOC): Located on elevated topography in the Housing Board area; highest municipal plateau, safe from urban drainage overflow.
- Government Arts & Science Degree College Campus: Elevated multi-story institutional complex on Nizamabad Road with ample open paved space for helicopter staging and disaster relief distribution.
- Kamareddy Government High School & Junior College Campus: Centrally located, sturdy two-story reinforced concrete structures serving as primary community shelter.
- Housing Board Colony Higher Tiers: Solid residential terrain above typical storm drain flood levels.

3. 24x7 HOSPITALS, MEDICAL HUBS & AMBULANCE DISPATCH (KAMAREDDY)
- Kamareddy District Headquarters Hospital (Government):
  Location: Station Road, Kamareddy.
  Facilities: 24x7 Emergency Trauma Wing, Intensive Care Unit (ICU), Blood Bank, Pediatric Emergency Care, and full stock of Polyvalent Anti-Snake Venom (ASV) and Rabies vaccines.
  Emergency Desk Phone: 08468-221100.
- Community Health Centre (CHC) & Maternal Wing:
  Location: Near Old Bus Station, Kamareddy.
  Facilities: Maternal delivery care, primary wound dressing, and stabilization before tertiary transfer.
- 108 Emergency Ambulance Command:
  Kamareddy district maintains 108 emergency advanced life support (ALS) ambulances staged at the District Hospital, Collectorate, and Devanpally junction.
