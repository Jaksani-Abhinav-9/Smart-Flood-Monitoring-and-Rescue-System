4. ESSENTIAL SCRIPT WHEN CALLING EMERGENCY SERVICES (WHAT CITIZENS MUST STATE)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When calling 112, 108, or 1077 during floods, citizens must stay calm and clearly state:
1. Exact Location: GPS coordinates (from FloodGuard app) OR nearest notable landmark, road junction, or school.
2. Current Water Situation: Water depth (ankle, waist, chest, roof level) and whether water is actively rising or steady.
3. Headcount: Total number of persons trapped, including how many infants, children, elderly, or bedridden individuals.
4. Immediate Medical Needs: Any injured, hypothermic, or diabetic/heart patients needing critical care.
5. Immediate Hazards: Live hanging electrical wires, smell of LPG gas, collapsing masonry, or sinking vehicles.
6. Battery Notice: Inform the operator: "I have X% phone battery remaining; I am keeping the line free after this report."
